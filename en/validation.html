D < 1: sub-exponential decay — heavy tail, natural regime. D > 1: super-exponential — compressed, accelerating collapse. D behaves as an order parameter for extraction: it doesn't prove causality, but it tracks the extraction regime with high consistency.

II. The prediction

If D is an order parameter for extraction, then interventions that reduce extraction should cause D to decrease. This is a directional, falsifiable prediction — not a post hoc interpretation.

Prediction: Reducing probe frequency (HFT, algorithmic feeds, pollution) should lower the fitted D in the affected system. Removing the intervention should raise D again.

If D does not respond to interventions — if it stays constant regardless of what we do — then D is merely descriptive, and DREAM is not a law. The test below examines four natural experiments.

III. Interventions: four natural experiments

★ Sweden FTT (1984–1991): the reversal test

Sweden imposed a financial transaction tax in 1984. Equity turnover dropped ~40%. The tax was repealed in 1991. Turnover returned.

During tax: D = 0.72 (below extraction threshold — turnover decayed slowly, extraction suppressed)
After repeal: D = 4.74 (deep into extraction — turnover shot back, re-extraction accelerated)

This is the strongest evidence. A reversible prediction — intervention lowers D, removal raises it — is much harder to dismiss than a one-way correlation. The system tracks the extraction regime in real time.

China minor gaming ban (2021)

China capped minors' gaming at 3 hours/week. Excessive gaming prevalence dropped from 28% to 4%.

Before ban: D = 4.15 (severe extraction)
After ban: D = 0.12 (natural regime)

Montréal Protocol (1987)

CFCs banned globally. Stratospheric ozone decline slowed, then stabilized.

Before ban: D = 1.27 (extraction — ozone declining super-exponentially)
After ban: D = 0.58 (natural — decline slowed to sub-exponential)

Volcker Rule (2012)

US banks banned from proprietary trading. Prop trading volume decayed over the following years.

Baseline financial D: ~1.5
Post-Volcker prop trading decay: D = 0.97 (at threshold — extraction suppressed)

Result: in all four cases, the estimated D moved downward during the intervention. In the Sweden case, D increased again after repeal. These first interventional tests are consistent with the hypothesis that D behaves as an order parameter for extraction.

IV. Future falsification

A scientific hypothesis is only credible if it specifies what would prove it wrong. The following observations would falsify or weaken the DREAM framework:

  1. Intervention that reduces extraction but D does not drop. If a transaction tax, feed regulation, or pollution control is enacted and measured D remains constant or rises, the order-parameter hypothesis fails.
  2. Natural system with D > 1. If any endogenous system (no external extractor) shows D > 1 with high R², the "extraction signature" interpretation collapses. Currently zero known cases.
  3. Extracted system where S2 does not fit. If a known extraction regime (HFT, social media, polluted ecosystem) shows R² < 0.5 for the S2 form while an alternative (power law, exponential) fits better, S2 is not the universal retention law.
  4. D > 1 that does not respond to intervention reversal. If an intervention is applied and removed, and D does not track the change (stays high during intervention, stays low after removal), D is not an order parameter — it's a coincidence.
  5. Cross-domain inconsistency. If D behaves as an order parameter in cognitive systems but not in financial or ecological systems, the "universality" claim fails and DREAM is domain-specific, not universal.

These tests are prospective. They can be run by any researcher with access to the relevant data. The framework predicts specific outcomes; alternative outcomes would falsify it.

V. Current limitations & research roadmap

These are not apologies — they are the open questions that define the research program:

  • Four intervention studies. The initial tests are consistent with the hypothesis, but four is not universal. Additional natural experiments are currently being collected: EU FTT proposals, India demonetization, Australia under-16 social media ban (2024), EU Digital Markets Act enforcement.
  • Two rely on sparse historical datasets. Sweden FTT and Volcker Rule have 5–8 data points each. The D estimates are directionally reliable but confidence intervals remain to be quantified via bootstrap or Bayesian methods.
  • Tick-level data needed for HFT. Daily/hourly price data averages out the microsecond probing that defines HFT. Order-book-level data (ITCH feeds, exchange websockets) would provide the full dynamic range needed for precise D measurement.
  • Causal mechanism not yet established. D correlates with extraction and responds to interventions, but the causal pathway (probe frequency → retention curve compression → D increase) is inferred, not directly measured. Longitudinal controlled studies would strengthen this.
  • Domain-specific calibration. The D → life-years mapping is calibrated for cognitive retention. Financial and ecological mappings need separate calibration against domain-specific harm metrics.

VI. Science and policy: a necessary separation

The scientific claim: D behaves as an order parameter for extraction, and responds directionally to interventions. This is an empirical observation, testable by anyone, and falsifiable by the criteria above.

The policy claim — that governments should regulate based on D — is not a consequence of the mathematics alone. It is a political decision. If repeated validation confirms that D is a reliable indicator of extraction, it could become a quantitative policy metric analogous to emissions limits, inflation targeting, or the unemployment rate. Whether governments choose to regulate based on that metric is ultimately a policy decision that depends on values, not equations.

This distinction is important. It makes the scientific work harder to dismiss as ideology, and it makes the political argument honest about what it is: a choice, informed by measurement, not dictated by it.
