Tech Communism Overview
Plan 2.0, registries, science, abundance. The technological base for abolishing private property.

What is Techno-communism

Techno-communism is a communist society whose technological base already exists. Real-time planning, predictive logistics, verifiable registries, scalable computation — all of this exists today. The largest corporations (Walmart, Amazon, Alibaba) already use ML-based demand planning, supply chain optimization, and distributed registries to manage their operations. What is missing is political will — to apply these instruments for meeting human needs, not maximizing profit.

The goal is simple: priority of human needs and measurable outcomes over speculative effects. The means of production (including means of cognitive extraction) must belong to all. The only path to survival is a total ban on private ownership of the means of production. Otherwise capital will burn the planet.

Plan 2.0: Real-Time Coordination

20th-century central planning (USSR) failed due to information deficit: planners did not have real-time data. Today, Walmart manages 10,000+ stores in real time through an ML demand system. Amazon optimizes supply chains with hourly precision. Alibaba processes 543,000 orders per second (Double 11, 2024). Planning 2.0 technology already works — in the private sector.

  • Resource allocation by telemetry: energy, logistics, housing, healthcare.
  • Public KPIs and objective functions aligned with needs, not profit.
  • Rolling horizons: 12 weeks, 12 months, 10 years, with stress scenarios.
  • Predictive logistics: demand forecasting 90+ days ahead (like Walmart Luminate).

Open Registry: Verifiable Accounting

Distributed ledger technologies (DLT/blockchain) make accounting verifiable for society. Budgets, tenders, and milestones — in an immutable journal. Programmable audits; asymmetric transparency: citizen privacy by default, power transparency on demand.

Estonia has used blockchain for government registries since 2012. Georgia — for land registration since 2016. Sweden — for land cadastre. The technology is proven. Scalable. Ready.

Science: Model → Simulate → Build

Open science + mission-driven funding outperforms rent-seeking marketing. Rewards follow measurable contribution, not monopoly on intellectual property. Quantum computing (qubits encode 2^N states) opens superlinear growth in optimization capacity. IBM, Google, China are already building quantum systems. In techno-communism, these systems belong to everyone.

Abundance: Basics Guaranteed

Basic goods — housing, food, healthcare, education, connectivity — are guaranteed. Rare resources — by contribution, measured through a TEC (labor-energy coefficient) system. Not equalization, but measurable justice. Wealth is not a right to others' labor, but the result of one's own contribution.

Global food production: 2.7 billion tons of grain per year (FAO, 2024) — enough for 10 billion people. 800 million go hungry. The problem is not production — it is distribution. Capitalism cannot distribute because the NPV of a hungry person is zero.
