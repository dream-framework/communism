Power to Altruists
Selection for service, not for greed. Scientific foundations and technological implementation.

Theoretical Foundation

Capitalism selects for power those who maximize profit — that is, those who are best at appropriating others' labor. This is negative selection: the more willing a person is to exploit, the higher they rise. Techno-communism demands the opposite: power for those who voluntarily accept the conditions of service. The antithesis of capitalism.

Scientific Foundations for Selection

Research shows: approximately 15% of the population are stable altruists (psychometric studies of prosocial behavior). Up to 60% are willing to serve under transparent rules and guarantees. This is not utopia — it is statistics. The question is not "do altruists exist" but "can we identify them and give them power."

  • Multi-level psychometrics: personality tests, prosocial behavior assessment, game-theoretic decisions (Dictator Game, Ultimatum Game, Public Goods Game).
  • fMRI of reactions to power/money: research by David Rock (NeuroLeadership Institute) and Tanya Deater-Deckard (NIMH) shows that service motivation vs reward motivation is distinguishable at the neural level.
  • Digital footprint analysis 10-15 years: behavioral patterns, trust, commitment fulfillment.

TEC as a Measure of Personal Value

The labor-energy coefficient (TEC) is an objective measure of a person's contribution to the public good. Calculated by an AI algorithm based on data about labor, public activity, and outcomes.

  • Basic TEC 5.0-6.0 — access to basic goods (housing, food, healthcare).
  • High TEC 7.0+ — access to rare resources and leadership missions.
  • TEC of managers is public. TEC of citizens is private.

Manager Selection System

  1. 10+ years of real contribution; TEC ≥ 7.0.
  2. Neuro-test of motives: fMRI + behavioral tests for egoism vs service.
  3. One year in a "sandbox" under AI audit: all decisions in an open registry.
  4. Public audit: open dossier and voting.
  5. Continuous monitoring: TEC recalculated quarterly. Drop below 7.0 — removal.

Asymmetric Transparency

Sacrifice of privacy — only for those in power. Citizens retain rights to private life. Managers do not. Every step, every decision, every resource allocation — in an immutable registry. This is not surveillance. It is a condition of service. Those who are not ready — do not take power.

Service Economy and Motivation

  • Base: 0.8 × median TEC — sufficient for a dignified life.
  • Multipliers: 1.2× for success, 1.5× for outstanding results, 0.7× for errors.
  • No stocks. No options. No appropriation of future flows.

Social Adaptation

Within a generation — stable trust, a culture of service instead of a cult of greed. This is a question of civilizational survival. Capitalism selects predators. Techno-communism selects servants. Selection determines the survival of the system.
